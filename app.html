Adaptive endurance training

Your plan changes when your week does.

Inertia builds a training block around your race date, then rebuilds it when you miss a session, travel, or turn up tired. Your fuelling targets move with it.

  • 01 Rebuilds, not remindersMiss two sessions and the plan reshapes around what is left. You see the reasoning and the diff before anything is applied.
  • 02 Reads what you actually didStrava activities land against the planned session, so next week is built on real numbers rather than intentions.
  • 03 Fuel follows the loadCalorie and macro targets track the week’s volume instead of a figure you set once in January and stopped believing.
