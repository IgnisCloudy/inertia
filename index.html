Most plans are written once, for an athlete who never has a bad Tuesday.

Then work runs late, a cold goes round the house, a flight gets moved, and by week five the plan and your life have stopped speaking to each other. You either follow a schedule that no longer fits, or you stop following one at all.

What happens when the week goes wrong

Nothing silent. Every change to your plan is proposed, explained, and shown as a before and after before it is applied.

  1. 01

    You describe the race and the life around it

    Distance, date, current volume, the days you can train, injuries to work around, and how much time you honestly have. Four steps, once.

  2. 02

    Inertia writes the block and shows its reasoning

    Phases, weekly volume, and every session down to the interval, with the thinking behind the structure written in plain language rather than coach shorthand.

  3. 03

    It reads what you actually did

    Completed sessions, skipped ones, and anything that landed in Strava. Planned against actual, session by session, with no manual logging.

  4. 04

    It rebuilds, and tells you exactly what changed

    When you have drifted far enough to matter, you get a diff. Old session on one side, new session on the other, and the reason it moved. You apply it or you do not.

Before you start

Questions worth asking first

Is this coaching or medical advice?

Neither. Plans are generated by a model and can be wrong. Inertia is not a substitute for a coach, a physiotherapist or a doctor, and you train at your own risk. You are asked to acknowledge this before your first plan is built.

Do I need a power meter or a heart rate strap?

No. Sessions are prescribed by effort in plain language, so you can run them on feel. If you do wear a monitor, the zone and split detail is there when you want it.

What happens if I miss a whole week?

The plan notices the drift and offers to rebuild around what is left before race day. Missed work is not simply piled onto next week, and nothing changes until you accept it.

Which sports does it cover?

Swim, bike, run and strength, as a single block rather than four schedules competing for the same week. Single sport goals work the same way.

Can I get my data out, or delete it?

Yes to both, from the menu, without writing to anyone. Download exports every row held about you as JSON. Delete removes those rows and clears local storage.

Are you tracking me?

No advertising, analytics or tracking cookies are set. Storage is used for your login session, your preferences and your coach history. Details in the cookie notice and the privacy policy.
